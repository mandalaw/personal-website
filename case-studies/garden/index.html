2021–2022 · Mississauga GIS project

Community Garden Site Analysis

Finding a place for a community garden

A possible garden site is more than an empty patch on a map. I used open data and spatial analysis to compare locations against the project’s criteria.

My part

I analyzed open data, organized geographic information in GIS databases and explained site-selection findings through maps and reports for city mentors and the project team.

Working with the data

The work combined statistics with spatial mapping. Shared criteria gave us a way to compare sites; the maps made it easier to see why their locations mattered.

What came out of it

The project produced analysis, maps and reports. It was a GIS project with city mentors, not City employment. I don’t have evidence that a recommendation was adopted.

What remains online

The original StoryMap is offline. The map shown here comes from the project archive. It provides context without pretending to be an interactive map.
